# Sergii Shpak

I build and take ownership of production web and mobile software, especially products that need clear architecture, reliable delivery, and AI-assisted development with real engineering judgment.

## What I work on

- Existing applications that need stabilization, documentation, production readiness, or reliable continuation.
- End-to-end web and mobile products, from the business model and user flows through implementation, deployment, and iteration.
- AI capabilities integrated into real workflows where permissions, data quality, fallback behavior, and human review matter.

## How I work

I use AI agents heavily, but not as a substitute for engineering ownership. I turn ambiguous requirements into a clear model, build in small end-to-end feature slices, and use tests, review loops, and product-level verification to keep delivery fast without making the next release fragile.

## Technology

TypeScript and Next.js, React, Supabase and PostgreSQL, Flutter, Python and FastAPI, OpenAI and Claude integrations, Docker, CI/CD, Sentry, webhooks, Stripe Connect, and production deployment.

## Client work

Most of my recent production repositories are private client work. I do not publish client code, credentials, or business logic without permission. For relevant conversations, I can share live products, client-safe walkthroughs, and the architectural and delivery decisions behind the work.
